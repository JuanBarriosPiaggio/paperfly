Privacy Policy

Paper Plane Drift (com.vexlo.paperflydrift)
Effective date: 4 August 2026
Last updated: 4 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Vexlo (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with the Android mobile game Paper Plane Drift (the “App”), available on Google Play.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who we are

Paper Plane Drift is published by Vexlo. For privacy questions, contact us at: privacy@vexlo.com (or via the developer contact email listed on the Google Play store listing).

2. Information we collect

We design the App to store most gameplay data only on your device. We do not create a separate account system for the game itself, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, or payment card details inside the App.

2.1 Information stored on your device

The App may store locally (using Android DataStore / app storage):

  • High scores, distance, and clean-glide streaks
  • Daily challenge progress
  • Unlocked and equipped plane skins
  • Settings such as sound on/off
  • Whether ads have been removed via an in-app purchase

This data stays on your device unless you clear the App’s data, uninstall the App, or a feature you choose (such as Google Play Games) syncs scores or achievements with Google.

2.2 Information collected by Google services

The App uses Google services that may collect and process information according to Google’s own policies. Depending on your device settings and region, this may include advertising identifiers, device information, purchase tokens, and Play Games profile data.

3. How we use information

Information is used to:

  • Operate core gameplay and remember your progress on the device
  • Show advertisements (unless you have purchased Remove Ads)
  • Process in-app purchases through Google Play Billing
  • Submit scores and unlock achievements via Google Play Games Services (when enabled)
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the App

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads using Google AdMob, including rewarded ads (for example, to continue after a crash or unlock certain skins), interstitial ads, and banner ads.

AdMob and its partners may collect information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type, and interaction with ads, to provide and measure ads and to help prevent fraud. On Android, the App may use the Advertising ID permission for this purpose.

You can limit ad personalisation in your device settings (for example, Google → Ads → “Opt out of Ads Personalisation”, or equivalent settings on your version of Android).

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy.

If you purchase Remove Ads, the App will stop showing ads while that purchase remains valid on your Google Play account / device entitlements.

5. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (such as Remove Ads or cosmetic skins) are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Google processes payments under Google’s terms and privacy policy. We receive purchase / entitlement information needed to unlock features you bought.

6. Google Play Games Services

If you use leaderboards or achievements, the App may use Google Play Games Services. That may involve signing in with your Google / Play Games account and sharing scores, achievements, and related game progress with Google so they can be shown on leaderboards and in your Games profile.

Use of Play Games is optional. You can manage Play Games settings in the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

7. Internet access

The App requests Internet permission to load ads, process purchases, and sync with Play Games when those features are used. Offline play of the core game is still possible when those network features are unavailable.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience arcade game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App or related Google services, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Parents and guardians should review Google’s family and advertising settings on the device.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • Local game data: remains on your device until you clear App storage or uninstall the App.
  • Ads / Billing / Play Games data: retained by Google according to Google’s policies. You may also use Google account tools to manage activity and advertising preferences.

To remove local App data: Android Settings → Apps → Paper Plane Drift → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the App.

10. International processing

Google and advertising partners may process data on servers located outside your country of residence. Where required by law (for example in the UK or EEA), such transfers are carried out under appropriate safeguards described in Google’s privacy documentation.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the UK GDPR / EU GDPR and similar laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal data, or to object to certain processing (including personalised advertising).

Because most App progress is stored only on your device, many requests are best handled by clearing App data or adjusting device / Google settings. For other requests related to our processing, contact privacy@vexlo.com. For data held by Google (ads, purchases, Play Games), you may also need to use Google’s privacy tools or contact Google directly.

12. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to an Android game that relies on Google Play services. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy, where permitted by law.
